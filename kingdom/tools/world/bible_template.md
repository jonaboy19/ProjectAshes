# Valencious world bible (Region 1: the Ashford Vale)

Phase W0 of `PA_plans/WORLD_BRIEF.md`. Generated by `kingdom/tools/world/build_bible.py` from the live WorldGen dump and the canon files; edit the tool or its inputs, not the tables.
Machine-readable twin: `docs/world/valencious_places.json`. Map: `docs/world/valencious_bible_map.png`. Raw dumps: `docs/world/dump_*.txt`, `dump_world_map.json`, `dump_r1_sites.json`.

## 1. Authority and how this file was made

- **Authoritative positions = the live game** at the commit this was built from: `WorldGen` (seed 1066) settlements, `RegionSites.plan` sites and the discovery places, dumped by `tools_qa/map/dump_world.gd` and `tools_qa/region1/world_dump.gd`.
- `docs/regions/WORLD_R1.md` and `assets/ui/maps/region1_parchment.json` (painted from WorldGen by `tools_qa/map/paint_parchment.gd`, covers only +-4096 m of the +-6144 m world) are **stale for the randomly placed sites**: 19 of their positions differ by more than 25 m from the current dump (section 12). Settlements, camps, the lore places and the Region 1 canon sites that have a fixed `search` block agree. The differing values are kept per place as `alt_pos` in the JSON.
- Merged sources: `WORLD_LORE.md` (nations), `REGION_1_PLAN.md` 1.2-1.7, `WORLD_R1.md`, `OWNER_DECISIONS.md`, `WORLD_VISION.md` 1-2, `LOOK_R1.md`, `data/world/{regions,first_region,nations}.json`, `data/region1/world/*.json`, `region1_parchment.json`, the posters `docs/art/regions/*.webp`, and Antigravity's showcase (inspiration only, section 13).
- Height is one function (`WorldGen.height`). This bible fixes *where things are and what the land should be like*; the macro heightmap (W1) only replaces the noise inside `_raw_height`.

## 3. Coordinate system

Metres from Ashford (0, 0). **x east, z south, north = -z.** The world is +-6144 m (12 288 m square); a 200 m rim rises on the outer 250 m. Raster maps (bible map, macro heightmap): row 0 = north edge, column 0 = west edge. The original valley is the +-2 km core; seed 1066; streamed in 64 m chunks.

## 10. Seasons and day feel

4 x 28 days (`seasons.gd`). Spring: green, floods, Ashrun high, Stagborn migrate north, Planting Festival. Summer: gold, fairs, Solkar caravans arrive, the Scar spreads fastest. Autumn: amber, harvest, Kindling Night. Winter: blue, scarcity, the north pass snows shut, the Scar goes dormant. **Snow line for terrain: above 180 m (the Northern Ridge).** Day: dawn mist on the Mere, market mornings on the Ember Road, noon fieldwork, dusk lantern gates, night wolves at the ward's edge.

## 13. Antigravity material (inspiration only, not adopted)

`7bdbc352` proposed a **32 km** world (`data/world/valencious_32km_sectors.json`, 53k lines of budget numbers, `tools/world/world_optimizer_32km.py`, 20 race GLBs); `fc8f73a7` added `docs/VALENCIOUS_WORLD_VIEWER.html` with AI concept art; the brain note `VALENCIOUS_WORLD_SHOWCASE.md` describes three showcase biomes: rural plains and valleys with enterable watermills, terraced wheat fields, stone bridges and pastures; ancient sacred redwood forests with rope bridges and mossy stone lanterns; northern mountain sects above cloud seas with pagodas and suspension bridges. None of it contains terrain or geography. **Decision: keep 12 288 m.** The three biome moods are kept as art direction: the Heartvale meadow/terrace look, dense old forest (Duskbriar, Greywood, Mistwood) and the high snow ridge.
