3D pixel-art outline/highlight post-process shader by Leo Peltola
Source: https://github.com/leopeltola/Godot-3d-pixelart-demo (MIT licence, see LICENSE)
Made for low-resolution 3D rendered with a pixel-art finish, like this game's renderer.
A perspective-camera variant (by rllyy97) is at https://godotshaders.com/shader/3d-pixel-art-outline-highlight-shader-perspective-camera/
