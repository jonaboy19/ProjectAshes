# Animals (ordinary, non-monster)

All 26 GLBs are free for commercial use: **CC0** (Quaternius sources plus our own procedural models). No credit is required.
Wolf, boar, bear, spider and wyvern are being made separately with Meshy, so they aren't here.

- **Previews:** `_previews/animals_sheet.png` shows everything to scale next to a 1.75 m human, in 3/4 view. `_previews/animals_sheet_small.png` is a close-up of the small animals.
- **Style:** one faceted low-poly look for every animal. All of them use **one shared 256 px texture** (`animals_atlas`, generated by `_tools/palette.py`). It is a warm hand-painted palette with a vertical light gradient (darker at the bottom, sunlit at the top) and faint brush noise. The texture is embedded in each GLB with a single material and flat shading.
- **Format:** GLB with no Draco or meshopt. The model faces glTF +Z (Godot `MODEL_FRONT`), the origin is on the ground between the feet, and units are real-world metres. Animations are separate named clips; set looping in Godot import (`Idle`, `Walk`, `Run`, `Eat`, `Swim`, `Fly`, `Glide`).
- **Rebuild:** run the scripts in `_tools/` with Blender 5.2 headless (commands are in each script header). `.gdignore` stays in place until the game uses the pack.

All models fit the mobile budget: small animals ≤ 1.5k tris, medium ≤ 3k, large ≤ 6k, texture ≤ 512 px, ≤ 30 bones. File sizes range from 160 to 1100 KB, and most of that is animation data.

## Processing notes
- **UAA rigs:** these had 38 to 51 bones. The IK, pole and foot helper bones were removed, with their hoof weights merged into the lower-leg bones. Ear tips (Ear3 and Ear4) and tail tips beyond Tail3 were merged into their parents. The baked clips play unchanged; see `_previews` for checks.
- **Farm sheep and pig:** these only came with Idle and Jump. Walk, Run and Death were copied by bone name from the same pack's cow (same template rig, with locations scaled by leg length). The FBX object-level keys were stripped.
- **Stray objects removed:** Quaternius glTFs contain a stray "Icosphere" (a bone display shape), which was deleted. Note that Blender's glTF importer adds a bone-shape Icosphere of its own when you re-import a file; that one isn't in the file.

## Candidates checked and rejected
Recorded so nobody re-evaluates them.

- **Existing but not used:**
  - `opengameart/models/chicken-animated` (161k tris) and `rooster-animated` (CC0, CDmir): photo-textured, clash with the faceted style.
  - `opengameart/models/dog-low-poly-rigged`: 47 bones and no clips.
  - `polypizza` chickens, pigs and horses: Quaternius voxel "cube" style or CC-BY Poly-by-Google statics.
  - `kenney/cube-pets`: toy style.
  - `3dassets-dev-ai` livestock and stable horses: AI-generated, static, blocky.
  - `lowpoly-animated-animals` Horse and Cow: superseded by the UAA versions.
- **Downloaded, not used:**
  - Quaternius 2023 atlas animals (Cat, Dog, Sheep, Pig, Chicken, Chick, Horse on Poly Pizza, CC0): voxel/cube style.
  - Quaternius cartoon Pigeon, Birb, Chicken and Cat (round, big-eyed).
  - Gobkit Free Animal Packs A and B (CC0; duck, goat, rat, owl): chibi toy style.
  - OpenGameArt raven-0, pigeon and rabbit (CC0): mixed quality and style, so replaced by procedural models.
- **Not allowed:** OpenGameArt "Low-poly Goat Family" is **CC-BY-SA 3.0**, which the licence gate excludes.

## Gaps / next steps
- The birds and rabbit are simple rigid-part rigs (one bone per body part) with procedural clips. They read well at gameplay distance but not in close-ups.
- There's no swimming animation for the duck or goose; use `Idle` on water.
- Fish have no mesh variation. For schools, tint them per instance in a shader.
- Horses have no saddle or bridle meshes. See `polypizza/cc-by/Saddle_*` and `3dassets-dev-ai/equestrian-yard-and-stables/saddle.glb`. The rider mount point is the `Body`/`Back` bone.
