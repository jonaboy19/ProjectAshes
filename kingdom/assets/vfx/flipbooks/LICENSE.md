# Flipbook licences

All six sheets in this folder are ORIGINAL work of the Rising Ashes project, generated by the scripts in
`tools/vfx/` (bake_gas_sims.py, bake_water_splash.py, bake_procedural.py). They contain no third-party
textures, images, or sprites.

| Sheet | Method | Atlas | Frames | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| fire_burst.png | Blender 5.2 Mantaflow fire sim, Cycles volume render | 1024x1024, 4x4 @ 256 px | 16 | project-owned (no third-party content) |
| smoke_puff.png | Blender 5.2 Mantaflow smoke sim, Cycles volume render | 1024x1024, 8x8 @ 128 px | 64 | project-owned |
| dust_burst.png | Blender 5.2 Mantaflow smoke sim, Cycles volume render | 1024x1024, 8x8 @ 128 px | 64 | project-owned |
| water_splash.png | Blender 5.2 Mantaflow FLIP liquid, Cycles toon shader | 1024x1024, 4x4 @ 256 px | 16 | project-owned |
| lightning_sheet.png | numpy procedural (midpoint-displacement bolts, distance-field glow) | 1024x1024, 4x4 @ 256 px | 16 | project-owned |
| magic_swirl.png | numpy procedural (log-spiral arms, warped value noise) | 1024x1024, 4x4 @ 256 px | 16 | project-owned |

Tools used to make them (not shipped): Blender (GPL; the licence does not extend to renders), Python/numpy (BSD).
Frame order is row-major, left to right, top to bottom.
