Distant Nations & Future Regions Blueprint (Handoff for Claude)

Documented in HANDOFF_VALENCIOUS_32KM_RACES.md so Claude and Codex can implement subsequent world expansions with distinct historical & fantasy archetypes:

Region Expansion A

Ancient Greek / Roman Empire (Solmarch & Aethelgard)

Fluted Marble Columns • Colosseums • Legion Shields

White marble pantheons, gladiatorial amphitheaters, stone aqueducts spanning valleys, and disciplined legionaries holding rectangular scutum shields.

Region Expansion B

Ancient Egypt / Desert Nile (Oasis of Amun-Ra)

Sandstone Obelisks • Hieroglyphic Pylons • Sun Temples

Vast river delta with golden dunes, monumental pharaoh statues, river felucca barges, and ancient underground tomb labyrinths.

Region Expansion C

Aetherial Sky Islands (The Shattered Heavens)

Floating Landmasses • Anti-Gravity Leylines • Sky Ships

Floating continental fragments held aloft by raw Aetherial gravity stones, cloud docks, cascading waterfalls pouring into the void, and winged avian aeries.

Region Expansion D

Submerged Water Cities (The Tide Empress Realm)

Coral Domes • Glass Tunnels • Bioluminescent Canals

Canal towns that partially submerge during high tides, connecting to deep-sea merfolk palaces protected by pressurized mana barrier domes.

Region Expansion E

Colossal Dwarven Cavern Kingdom (Hollowdeep / Karnhollow)

10km Underground Cave • Molten Lava Aqueducts • Anvil Statues

Built entirely inside a colossal 10-kilometer subterranean mountain cavern. Giant stalactites carved into multi-tier fortresses, glowing molten metal aqueducts, and massive basalt forge halls.
