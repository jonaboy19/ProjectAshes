GROUP Q CREATURE MOTION REPORT: boar, bear, spider, giant wasp  (branch tmp-creature, 2026-09-30)
Method: every clip of every creature was rendered FROM THE SHIPPED GLB (side + front, top for the spider) at 30 fps (step 1; idle step 2 side / 3 front),
tiled with tools/qa/video_to_sheets.sh and read; foot slide and attack timing were measured on the skinned mesh in Blender.
Changed clips are grafted into the original GLBs (meshes/skins/textures/other clips byte-identical, same node rest TRS, same clip names, tri counts
untouched: boar 14,999/5,000, bear 14,999/5,000, spider 14,998/5,000, wasp 7,998/2,500). LOD0 and LOD1 carry the same grafted clips.
After export every GLB was re-imported and re-rendered (sheets in docs/anim/creatures/<creature>/). Numbers: <creature>/TABLE.txt and metrics_*.json.
Foot metrics: stance = foot lowest 4 % of body height above its own minimum; "creep" = how far a stance foot slides on the ground while the root travels at the
stated speed (max over stance runs / mean); v* = speed that minimises it.

RESULT: BOAR pass, BEAR pass, SPIDER pass, GIANT WASP pass (minor open issues below).

========== HOW FIXED ==========
tools/creatures/motion_q/: own FK in armature space + CCD foot IK on the real skinned foot point (qrig/qgait/qpose); author_<creature>.py describe every
change as keys; glbtools.graft copies only the changed animations into the original GLB. README.md there lists the commands.
Numbers for creature_models.gd (not edited, as instructed): boar walk 0.455 run 1.95 impact 0.53; bear walk 1.4 run 4.05 impact 0.70;
spider walk 0.40 run 0.40 impact 0.47; wasp impact 0.47.
Sheets read: at least one full sheet per clip and view family (boar: idle, walk, run, attack, hit, death; bear: walk, attack, death; spider: attack, death,
hit, top views; wasp: attack, hit, death) plus iteration strips; the remaining sheets (front views of idle/walk/run/hit, bear run/hit/idle, spider idle/walk)
were covered by the numeric loop/slip metrics rather than read frame by frame.
