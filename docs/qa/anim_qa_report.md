## Visual review and judgement (2026-09-27, local GPU session)

Sheets reviewed: `anim_sheet_villagers.jpg`, `anim_sheet_armored.jpg`, `anim_sheet_creatures.jpg`, `anim_sheet_animals.jpg`, plus the worst strips in `anim_strips/`. Locomotion strips move the model at the **game** speed with the camera following, so a planted foot should stand still against the grid.

**Overall: the clips themselves mostly look natural; what makes the game look robotic is how they are played.**

* **Humanoid clips (UAL) are good motion.** Idle breathes, the walk has a proper heel-strike / toe-off and hip sway, the jog has a real flight phase, and the sword swings have anticipation and follow-through. Baked foot slip in the walk is small (4-20 cm/s, mostly from foot roll). Idle, Blocking, 2H idle, sit and talk loops pass on nearly every character, with no pops and clean loop seams.
* **In game they ice-skate.** Every walking human is driven 1.6-2x faster than the walk clip's stride covers (player 4.2 m/s against about 2.1 m/s; villagers 1.6 against 0.98, and 4.8 m/s during catch-up). In the strips the feet visibly drag across the grid. This is the single most visible problem, and it is in game code (see below).
* **Long dresses and robes stretch in Running_A / Dodge_Forward / Hit_B.** villager_woman_a/b, mother, elder_woman, child_girl and cdmir_old_lady have a skirt that tents into a sharp wedge between the legs on wide strides. It is fine in walk and idle. This is skinning (the skirt is weighted to the thighs), so it matters for NPCs that run or flee, not for the player.
* **Meshy armored boots (FIXED).** Before the fix, the boots and sabatons of all 6 armored characters were weighted to the pelvis and thighs. They smeared into long flat blades whenever a foot lifted, sank up to about 20 cm into the floor, and slid about 40 cm/s. They now follow the feet (see "What was fixed"). The knight and noble cloak hems still clip into the floor a little in the run.
* **Death_A is now a different clip.** `Assets.UAL_FILES` now includes `UAL_Extra_Mesh2Motion.glb`, which has its own `Death_A`/`Death_B`, and those win over the `Death_A -> Death01` alias. The new clip is a 4.5 s stagger-kneel-fall. It ends about 0.7 m ahead of where the body started, with the arms under the chest (reported as 14-57 cm "below floor" on bulky meshes; in the strips the body does rest on the ground). It reads well but is long.
* **Hit_B (Hit_Knockback) and Dodge_Forward** leave the ground for 0.3-0.7 s and travel with baked motion. That is by design (a knockback and a roll), but on robe and armored meshes the roll pushes cloth 20-30 cm through the floor.
* **Quaternius goblins (CampMonster).** The style is simple but the motion is readable. `Run` barely touches the ground: the feet hover 3-5 cm and contact is never detected, so its speed shows as "inf". `Punch` stretches the shoulder by 94 % (a position key on Shoulder.R). The Foot and IK helper bones carry translation keys. Combined with the game speeds (walk 2.2x and run about 3x the stride), goblins skate badly.
* **Quaternius wolf (Wolf).** The clips are good, but the wolf is driven 2.5-5.5x too fast (the 3.5 m/s "stalk" plays on the Walk clip), so it glides.
* **Meshy creatures (not wired yet).** Goblin, orc and wolf look good. The troll walk has a very long stride and wide feet: the contact estimate is noisy (631 cm/s slip), but it reads as a heavy stomp. The bear and wolf quadruped rigs (Quaternius retarget) slide in walk and run, as their README warns. The spider and wyvern are procedural: readable but mechanical. The troll's attack pushes its hands 33 cm into the floor (documented in its README).
* **Animals.** The Quaternius farm animals and deer look natural, but the walk and run speeds are mostly wrong, in both directions. Horses, donkeys, cows and oxen walk slower than their clip (0.5-0.7x, a treadmill look). Dogs, foxes and pigs run 1.5-3x too fast. The small procedural animals (chicken, duck, goose, pigeon, crow, rabbit) have hop or waddle cycles with tiny strides, so any game speed reads as sliding. Sheep and pig `Run`/`Walk` have a front foot bone that stretches 60-130 % (a retarget artifact that shows as a rubbery leg).

## What was fixed at the asset level

* `kingdom/assets/incoming/ai3d/meshy/armored/{guard,knight,mercenary,bandit,noble,orc_warchief}{,_lod1}.glb`: the foot and boot-shaft vertex weights were rebound to `foot_*`/`calf_*` by `tools/qa/anim_qa/fix_armored_feet.py`, with a small toe share and a smooth blend into the existing weights above the ankle. The skeleton, rest pose, mesh, UVs and material are unchanged (rest pose identical within 0.04 deg), and the export uses the same GLB settings as `armored_rig.py`. Guard results: Running_A penetration went from 18.3 to 1.0 cm, Walking_A slip from 41 to 4 cm/s and hover from 2.1 to 0 cm, and the boots no longer stretch (`anim_strips/guard__Running_A.jpg`). **Root cause:** the "skirt" rule in `tools/meshy/armored_rig/armored_rig.py` also catches wide boots near the floor. Run `fix_armored_feet.py` after any re-rig, or exclude `z < ankle` from that rule.
* No other assets were changed. The remaining problems are in game code (speeds, alias shadowing) or need re-authoring (skirt weights, the goblin Punch shoulder key, the sheep and pig foot retarget).

## Thresholds and why

| check | PASS | WARN | FAIL | why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| foot slide (p90 slip of a planted foot, cm/s) | < 8 | < 20 | >= 20 | A foot is 'planted' when it is within 2.5 cm of its lowest height in the clip. For in-place locomotion the slip is measured against the clip's own treadmill speed, so this is sliding baked into the clip; speed mismatches with the game are a separate table. ~8 cm/s is about where slip becomes visible at third-person distance; 20 cm/s reads as skating. |
| ground penetration (cm) | < 2 | < 5 | >= 5 | Lowest skinned vertex (feet-region vertices plus a 900-vertex sample of the body) or foot joint below y = 0. 2 cm hides in grass/terrain; 5 cm visibly sinks a boot or clips a body through the floor. |
| hover (cm) | < 3 | < 6 | >= 6 | The lowest foot never gets closer than this to the floor during a standing / moving clip: the character floats. |
| airborne (s) | < 0.20 | < 0.35 | >= 0.35 | Both feet > 5 cm above their contact height in a row, in non-jump clips. A real run has ~0.1 s flight per stride; > 0.2 s looks floaty. |
| bone stretch (%) | < 1 | < 5 | >= 5 | Joint-to-joint distance vs. rest. Skeletal animation should be rigid; >1% means position keys fight the character's proportions (retarget leftovers). |
| scale keys (%) | < 1 | < 5 | >= 5 | Any bone scaled away from 1 (e.g. Meshy's hips x1.18) inflates the mesh. |
| pops | 0 | 1-3 | > 3 | Frame where a major bone's angular acceleration exceeds 25 deg/frame^2 at 30 fps and is 6x that bone's p90 in the clip: a one-frame snap, not a fast but smooth swing. Fingers, leaf and IK helper bones are ignored. |
| loop seam | < 2 deg and < 1 cm | < 8 deg / < 3 cm | above | Pose jump when a looping clip wraps (last frame -> first frame), minus one ordinary frame step of that bone, max over major bones; plus the same for the hips height. What is left is a visible hitch every cycle. |
| root drift, in-place loops (cm) | < 3 | < 8 | >= 8 | The hips travel this far over one loop and snap back at the seam (the game moves the node, not root motion). |
| one-shot displacement (cm) | < 40 | >= 40 | | A one-shot with baked travel moves the body away from its collider and snaps back when the next clip starts (death is exempt). |
| T-pose frames | 0 | 1 | >= 2 | Frames whose mean major-bone rotation is within 4 deg of the bind (T) pose. |
| loop flag | looping | | not looping | Idle / walk / run / sit-idle / talk clips must be set to loop or they freeze on the last frame. |
| static idle | > 0.5 deg | <= 0.5 deg | | An idle loop that barely moves reads as a mannequin. |
| speed ratio (game / clip) | 0.85-1.18 | 0.70-1.40 | outside | 15-20% mismatch is hard to see; beyond 40% the feet visibly skate or moonwalk. |
