# Region 1 world layout (packages C0, C1, C2, C10, C11)

Done 2026-09-30 by the cloud session. Data: `kingdom/data/region1/world/` (`settlements.json`, `sites.json`, `creatures.json`), generated by
`kingdom/tools/region1/gen_world_data.py` (edit the script, re-run, commit the JSON). Code: `scripts/world/region1_world.gd` (site planner, one hook line at the end
of `RegionSites.plan`, after every older site so no id or position moved), `region1_extras.gd` (name boards, named NPCs, people, interior doors, snow drifts),
`region1_identity.gd` (rumours, aliases), `region1_creatures.gd` (C11). `RegionDressing` gained the asset keys `free:<cat>/<name>@H` (meshy_free) and
`r1:<dir>/<name>[@H]` (incoming/region1, Highwatch atlas material applied), an optional 5th part element (metres above ground) and `site.season`.
QA: `Godot --headless -s res://tools_qa/region1/world_dump.gd` lists the sites; `tools_qa/region1/world_views.json` feeds `look_capture.gd` (screens in `docs/regions/world/`).
Switch `--r1worldoff` drops the whole package (A/B).

## Canon decisions
- Names follow OWNER_DECISIONS: `caldrenn` shows as **Kingdom of Valencious** (dynasty House Caldrenn), `solmarch` as **Aurelis Patriarchate** (direction east); the other realms get `region_name`. Varska, Serathi, Solkar, Frostcrown and Umbrafen are `teaser_realms` in `nations.json`, deliberately NOT in `nations` (RAWorldLore needs mutual stances for every nation; war and factions read `nations`).
- **Silverford = Ironmarch** (town id 12, on the Silverrun, 474 m from the river), **Greenhollow = Oakvale**, Highwatch Keep stands 250 m north-east of Highcliff on the ridge. `WorldGen.display_name()` is the one canonical player-facing name (HUD location line, discovery banner, map, news, screens, town-kit dialogue and quests; `Discovery.display_name` delegates to it); WorldGen names stay the ids (saves, door ids, `settlement:<name>`, town-kit files). `tests/test_settlement_names.gd` pins it.
- The look agent's Stagborn Glade, Crownstead Mill Hill (with its Elder Stone), Wyrm's Ribs and Hollin's Reach are referenced, not duplicated.
- Lore rows for every story place id of `r1_registry.json` are in `first_region.json` (hidden helpers), so `region1_places.gd` resolves them. Note for the quest agent: `rift_mouth` currently resolves to "The Rift" by name; the finale mouth is `scar_arena` / "Scar Mouth Arena" (lore row `rift_mouth` has the right position: use `set_override` or drop "The Rift" from NAMES).

## Creatures (C11)
Species rows were added to `monster_ecology.gd` (SPECIES, `MAX_ALIVE_DENS` 48 to 72), `wolf.gd` (SPECIES) and `creature_models.gd` (MODELS): `stagborn_elk`, `stagborn_warden`, `ghoul`, `giant_wasp`, `bog_toad`, `rift_slime`, `rift_wraith`. FrontierPresence spawns them unchanged (den species = model key). Stats and clip speeds are first estimates: Codex tunes (X3 Stagborn behaviour, X4). Stagborn start no fights and flee when hurt; the Warden is an optional guardian (aggro 0, no gate). `Region1Creatures` seeds dens once per species (old saves get them too), walks the two herds north in spring and back by autumn (`herd_migration` ecology event), and gives each bandit camp a Squad roster that returns 6 days after it is wiped out. Scar variants of ordinary species stay the C4 hook (H4).
