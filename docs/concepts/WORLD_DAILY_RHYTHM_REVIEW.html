Why it can look staged: when a phase boundary changes, every simulated person in an affected job group gets a new target. WorldSim processes up to 1,500 people each frame, so a large group can begin its trip within a short burst. Targets are deterministic; work/home points repeat, and market points only vary by day. The display doesn't prove a runtime crowd failure; it makes the source schedule and its synchronization visible.
