Combat pressure window review

A small editable design model for thinking about simultaneous enemy attacks, impact delays, and player protection windows. It helps frame a fairness discussion; it does not simulate Rising Ashes or predict actual damage.

The independent-attacker rows use a simple evenly spaced cadence to make overlap visible. Real code randomizes cooldowns and also has approach distance, target movement, blocks, missed attacks and other constraints. Slot timing is schematic and does not imply a recommended final cadence. No hit probability or expected DPS is calculated.

Source facts represented

  • Wolf bite: 9 damage, 0.25 s delayed check, randomized cooldown 1.2–1.8 s.
  • Player: 0.35 s hurt cooldown and 0.35 s dodge invulnerability during a 0.45 s dodge.
  • These are read from the reviewed source snapshot; verify against the current gameplay branch before using them as implementation values.
