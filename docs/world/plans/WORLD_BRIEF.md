## Phase W1 — macro heightmap + splat generator, Terrain3D import, height adapter
Fixed: world 12 288 m (±6144); Terrain3D vertex spacing 1.5 m; region 256 px = 384 m, 32×32; macro heightmap 4097×4097 16-bit PNG, 3 m/px, range −20…+300 m (`h = −20 + v/65535·320`).
**W1a** `kingdom/tools/world/gen_valencious.py` (deterministic seed 1066, numpy/scipy only, `py -I`). Inputs: `docs/world/valencious_places.json`, `kingdom/data/world/valencious_regions.json` (from W0: id, name, polygon, h_min, h_max, roughness, biome weights). Algorithm: per-region base height smoothly blended over 400 m; FBM + ridged noise (numpy) scaled by roughness; massifs masked by region; carve Ashrun + Silverrun valleys from the dump polylines (300 m wide, ~15 m deep, monotonically falling downstream; fine channel stays WorldGen's); basins at the Mere and Westfen; hold 600 m around Ashford and Kingsreach within ±8 m of current `_raw_height` (sample it via a dump); keep the 200 m edge rim; `scipy.ndimage.gaussian_filter` σ 1–2. Outputs `kingdom/data/world/macro/`: `height_16.png`, `biome_ids.png` (8-bit, 1024²), `splat_weights.png` (RGBA 1024²), `preview.png` (hillshade + places 2048²), `meta.json` (size, m_per_px, h range, seed, sha256 of inputs). `--verify` re-runs and asserts byte-identical.
**W1b** `kingdom/scripts/world/world_macro.gd` (`class_name WorldMacro`): load `height_16.png` once into a uint16 `PackedByteArray` (~34 MB) in `WorldGen.setup()` on the main thread; `sample(x, z)` bilinear, read-only/thread-safe. Flag `--world_macro=noise|valencious`, env `ASHES_WORLD_MACRO`, `user://settings.cfg [world] macro`, same resolution order as `TerrainBackend`; default `noise` until W5 sign-off. `world_gen.gd _raw_height`: one-line hook `# Valencious macro hook` — if `WorldMacro.active`, base = `WorldMacro.sample(x, z) + _detail noise*0.6`, still through `hidden_valley.shape()`. Settlement/camp/clearing `base_h` follow automatically.
**W1c** Re-run `tools_qa/region1/bake_valley.gd` with the macro on; capture and read Hollin's Reach — measure fit.
**W1d** Texture slots 5 → 10 in `terrain3d_bridge.gd` + `pack_ground_textures.gd`: keep 0 grass, 1 forest floor, 2 path, 3 rock, 4 cobble; add 5 meadow/flower grass, 6 farmland/tilled soil, 7 marsh mud/wet, 8 rift ash (violet-black), 9 snow/scree.
