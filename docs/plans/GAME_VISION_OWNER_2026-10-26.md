# Rising Ashes — owner's game vision (2026-10-26, in the owner's words, organised)

Read this before designing or building any gameplay. It complements `READY_V1.md` and `docs/future/WORLD_VISION.md`.

## Core
- **It is also a survival game.** You gather your own resources (wood, stone, food, herbs, ore, hides…), craft and build. Survival needs matter.
- **You start in the village where you were born** (Ashford). The start is full of opportunities to move into — not one fixed path.
- **Where you spend the most time shapes your elements.** The place you hang around most is where your elemental affinity grows fastest (e.g. near the river/forge/forest/mountain → that element develops faster). Element growth is tied to location and time spent.
- **The Academy:** once you get into the Academy you choose the path you want to follow. Alternatively you can become an apprentice / take an apprenticeship instead of (or before) the Academy.
- **Ranks in every profession:** you work your way up through the ranks of any profession or job you choose (military, farmer, merchant, smith, mage, assassin, guild trades…). See `docs/future/WORLD_VISION.md` §4 for the career ladders.

## NPCs are the heart of the game
- NPCs are **not regular NPCs**: they must give a **full-dive experience** — personalities, memory, relationships, schedules, opinions, ambitions (see WORLD_VISION §3, §5).
- **Recruiting** is central: recruit people for specific purposes, in each profession or job you choose.
- **Apprentices:** you can take on apprentices yourself (teach them your profession; they grow and can work for you).
- **Companions:** travelling companions with their own stories, opinions, loyalty (C6/C7 started this).
- NPCs are used for many other things: workers, soldiers, settlers, traders, spies, healers, builders.

## Owning things and rising
- **Buy things:** shops, houses, workshops, land and many other medieval properties; run a shop/business.
- **War:** go to war, rise through the military ranks, earn land.
- **Form your own kingdom** — possible but with real risks (rivals, rebellion, war, succession). Path: camp → village → lordship → land → kingdom.
- And many other medieval mechanics (trade, taxes, laws, nobility, guilds, crime, religion…).

## Rules for builders
- Keep using the owner's GPU (local laptop session) for all visual/animation verification; cloud agents do code/headless work.
- Everything ties back to NPCs and player choice; avoid single linear paths.
- Ready-v1 scope still applies for the first release (`READY_V1.md`): the items above that aren't in v1 (Academy paths, apprenticeships, element-by-location, buying shops/houses, war ranks, kingdom) must be designed so v1 systems don't block them; implement the v1 subset first, then these in order of player impact. Several already exist as sims (careers, nobility, lordship, armies, realm modules) — reuse them.
